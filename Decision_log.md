# Decision Log – Car Price Prediction

## Decision 1: Dataset Selection
I selected a car price dataset containing vehicle-related information because factors such as manufacturing year, kilometers driven, fuel type and transmission can influence a car's selling price.

## Decision 2: Feature Selection
I chose multiple input features instead of relying on a single feature because car prices depend on several vehicle characteristics.

## Decision 3: Data Preprocessing
I performed data cleaning and preprocessing to handle numerical and categorical features and prepare the dataset for machine learning.

## Decision 4: Model Evaluation
I used MAE, MSE, RMSE and R-squared to evaluate prediction errors and understand how well the regression model predicts car prices.
